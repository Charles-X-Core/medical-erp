## Context

The project needs a clear hierarchy for documentation authority and conflict resolution.

## Decision

Document hierarchy is: AGENT.md > documentation-governance.md > docs/ > .agent/ > templates/ > logs/

## Consequences

### Positive

- Clear authority levels
- Predictable conflict resolution
- Prevents contradictory documentation

### Negative

- Requires understanding of hierarchy

### Neutral

- Establishes documentation governance
